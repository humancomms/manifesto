Policies and manifestos exploring human authorship, judgment, communication quality, and boundaries around AI use.

These projects overlap with Human Comms in different ways. They are listed for context, not as endorsements or research evidence.

Clay AI Writing Policy

Focuses on ownership of ideas and sentences, clear writing, and not shifting the cost of low-effort AI output onto readers.

John Hewitt's Lab Policy on AI in Writing & Communication

Requires human first drafts, readiness for a reasonable first follow-up, disciplined information quantity and quality, and ownership of technical terminology.

The HumAIne Manifesto

A broader human-centered AI manifesto emphasizing purposeful use, active thinking, human agency, and preserving human qualities over efficiency-at-all-costs.

No AI Movement

A broader movement protecting spaces for human work, creativity, direct contact, and interpersonal connection. Its scope is substantially wider than Human Comms.
